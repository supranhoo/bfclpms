# ADR-347 — Derived cycle anchors must lock every non-terminal month

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2 September 2026
- **Related:** ADR-087, ADR-296, POLICY §54, POLICY §128, POLICY §CONSOLE-FREQUENCY-DUE

## Context
In the Performance Console (September 2026), two Bi-Monthly KPIs under the same
KRA behaved differently: "Enhance Campaign life of 3x100" showed the
`Not due · Bi-Monthly (Sep-Oct)` chip while "Enhance Campaign life of 1050 TPD"
showed none. Both are Bi-Monthly; the only difference was the anchor stored in
`kpis.frequency_cycle_start` — `Jan-Feb` (a hardcoded option) vs `Jul-Aug`
(not in `BI_MONTHLY_OPTIONS`).

## Root cause (5-Why)
1. Why no chip? `resolveKpiDueState` returned `due: true` for the Jul-Aug KPI.
2. Why? `isKpiLockedForPeriod` found September absent from `lockedMonths`.
3. Why? The anchor is not a hardcoded option, so the option was synthesized by
   `deriveCycleOptionFromCycleStart`.
4. Why did that miss September? It emitted only the **first** window
   (`{'Jul-Aug': [7]}`) instead of the repeating rhythm across the year.
5. Why did nobody catch it? Existing tests only exercised anchors whose first
   window contained the month under test.

The DB trigger (`enforce_frequency_lock_on_submission`) uses modulo math and was
always correct, so this was a client-side display divergence only.

## Decision
`deriveCycleOptionFromCycleStart` now enumerates every cycle window in the
12-month rotation and locks all non-terminal months of each window. Behaviour for
hardcoded option anchors is unchanged.

## Consequences
- The "Not due" chip, Org KPI entry windows, Bulk Review due filter and every
  other consumer of `isKpiLockedForPeriod` now agree with the DB trigger for
  off-list anchors (`Jul-Aug`, `May-Jun`, `Feb-Apr`, `Nov-Apr`, …).
- No schema, RPC or write-path change; presentation/predicate only.
- Regression guard: `src/tests/derivedCycleAnchorLocking.test.ts`.

## Rollback
Revert `deriveCycleOptionFromCycleStart` in `src/lib/frequencyCycleOptions.ts`.
