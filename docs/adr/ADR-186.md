# ADR-186 — Bulk System-KPI uploads must cover mid-workflow reviews

- **Date:** 2026-07-27
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Policy:** POLICY §AR-SYSTEM-SLOT-COVERAGE
- **Reported as:** "101676 and 100508 — Annual Production Target vs. Actual data is not where it should be."

## Context

Both employees' `Annual Production Target Vs Actual` slot (`sys_3jsce5p`,
weight 25, template `408ae1b3…`) was empty while every peer on the same
template carried `raw = 91 → 15 pts` from the 25-Jul bulk upload.

On 25-Jul they were mid-workflow because of their ADR-185 re-open:
`101676` at `pending_bu`, `100508` at `pending_dept`.

## Root cause (5 Why)

1. Why is the slot empty? The 25-Jul bulk upload skipped both rows.
2. Why skipped? `parseAndDryRun` allowed only `STAGE_SAFE`
   (`not_started` / `pending_self` / `pending_manager`) or — with the ADR-171
   opt-in — `completed`.
3. Why did that miss them? Every status between those two worlds
   (`pending_skip`, `pending_dept`, `pending_bu`, `pending_hr`,
   `pending_management`) was an uncovered gap.
4. Why did nobody notice? The rows were reported as the generic
   `Locked stage: …` and rolled into one aggregate "N skip" badge — 1597 skips
   on that run, so a two-employee cohort was invisible.
5. Why did the score drop? `annual_review_compute_final_summary` builds the
   denominator from TEMPLATE weights, so a missing weighted slot scores
   **0 of 25** — it is not excluded. Totals were depressed to 51.00 / 58.20.

## Decision

1. **Repair (done).** Applied the peer value (`raw 91 → 15 pts`) to both
   instances and recomputed via `annual_review_compute_final_summary`.
   Before-state snapshotted in
   `annual_review_missing_system_slot_repair_2026_07` (`performed_by = NULL`,
   admin-read only) for rollback.

   | Employee | Before | After |
   |---|---|---|
   | 101676 Satyaban Roy | 58.20 Average | **73.20 Good** |
   | 100508 Satyam Kumar Jha | 51.00 Poor | **66.00 Average** |

2. **Prevent.** `src/lib/annualReview/bulkStageCoverage.ts` becomes the single
   classifier for upload stage coverage:
   - mid-workflow rows are still skipped by default, but with a
     **status-specific** reason;
   - a new admin opt-in, *Apply to mid-workflow reviews (upgrades only)*,
     routes them through the same monotonic, audit-logged
     `admin_apply_system_scores_upgrade` RPC used for completed rows —
     never a direct table write, never an eligibility write;
   - the dry-run report carries `stageStatus` on every row and a
     `skipsByStatus` breakdown that the dialog renders as per-stage badges.

## Outcome

Detector (weighted template slot with no stored value) after the repair — the
two reported instances are clear. Remaining, reported not swept:

| Cohort | Instances |
|---|---|
| `completed` missing *Annual Maintenance PM Target vs. Actual* | 86 |
| unnamed weighted slots (`pending_management` / `pending_dept` / `pending_self`) | 14 |
| assorted slots at `pending_bu` | 10 |

These are separate data-entry cohorts, not workflow defects; per
§AR-REOPEN-REQUIRES-TERMINAL-RESUBMIT they are handled by an explicit,
whitelisted upload — never a status-only sweep.

## CAPA

- **Corrective:** two instances repaired, audited, reversible.
- **Preventive:** `classifyStageCoverage` + `summariseSkipsByStatus` with
  regression coverage in `src/test/annualReview/bulkStageCoverage.test.ts`,
  which locks that a mid-workflow status is named explicitly and that the
  ADR-171 completed opt-in does **not** silently cover it.

## Rollback

`annual_review_missing_system_slot_repair_2026_07` holds prior
`system_scores`, `system_scores_raw`, `total_score` and `final_rating`; a
single update restores both instances. The UI change is additive — the new
checkbox defaults to off, reproducing pre-ADR behaviour exactly.
