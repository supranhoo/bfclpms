# ADR-188 — Monthly KRA detail sheet in the Annual Review report

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-28
- Policy: §RPT-MONTHLY-KRA-SHEET

## Context

Employees mapped to a KRA-driven annual-review template (a template whose
criteria pool is replaced by a `carry_kra` system slot) are scored entirely
from their rolled-up monthly KPI performance. The comprehensive report showed
only the final aggregate (`KRA Points`, `Rating Derived From = With KRA`), so
reviewers and HR could not see **which months** produced that number, or
whether a low annual score was caused by a genuinely weak month or by missing
monthly submissions.

## Decision

Add a **"Monthly KRA Scores"** sheet to the comprehensive report workbook.

- Scope: one row per instance whose resolved template carries a `carry_kra`
  slot. Non-KRA employees are excluded; if no KRA employees exist the sheet is
  omitted entirely.
- Columns: identity (code, name, designation, department, business unit,
  template), then **two columns per fiscal month** (July → June) — `<Mon> /5`
  (weighted monthly rating) and `<Mon> %` (achievement against the weighted
  maximum) — then `Months Scored`, `Avg /5`, `KRA Points`, `KRA Weight`.
- A month with no scored KPI is left **blank**, never `0`, so "no data" cannot
  be misread as a zero rating.
- The **Employees** sheet additionally carries a `KRA Months Counted` column
  (immediately after `KRA Points`): the number of fiscal months with a scored
  KPI behind that employee's KRA score. Blank for non-KRA templates. It is
  derived from the *same* matrix as the monthly sheet (`monthsScored`), fetched
  once per export, so the two surfaces cannot disagree. It is deliberately not
  re-derived from `kra_points`/`kra_weight`, which are a submission-time
  snapshot.

### Aggregation

New RPC `get_annual_review_monthly_kra_matrix(p_employee_ids uuid[],
p_fy_start int, p_exclude_na boolean)` — `STABLE SECURITY DEFINER`,
`search_path = public`, executable by `authenticated` but guarded to
`admin` / `hr_pms` / `management` (the report audience). It is the set-based
form of `compute_carry_kra_contribution`: weighted average of the authoritative
score (`final → auditor → manager → self`), `is_na` rows excluded, bucketed by
the 12 fiscal months of `p_fy_start`.

A per-employee client loop over `fetchMonthlyKraScores` was rejected: it is one
round-trip per employee (2,000+ for a full cycle) and reproduced the
`BUG-CARRY-TIMEOUT` failure mode.

## Consequences

- Read-only. No schema change, no writes, no RLS change. Rollback = drop the
  RPC and remove the sheet append.
- Client batches employee ids 500 per RPC call and caps the sheet at 5,000 rows.
- The sheet fails soft: an RPC error omits the sheet rather than aborting the
  whole workbook download.
- `Avg /5` may differ from `KRA Points / KRA Weight × 5` by ≤ ~0.05 because each
  month is rounded to 2 dp before averaging, and because `system_scores` holds a
  snapshot taken at submission time while the sheet reads live monthly data.
  Verified on employee 101148: sheet-derived 58.34 vs
  `compute_carry_kra_contribution` 58.31.
- Regression coverage: `src/test/annualReview/monthlyKraSheet.test.ts`.
