# ADR-278 — Console KPIs expand inline under their KRA

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 16 August 2026
- **Related:** ADR-259, ADR-264, ADR-268, ADR-270, ADR-273, ADR-277

## Context
Selecting a KRA rendered the KPI list as a separate card below the whole KRA
list, so the result of a click appeared far from the row that was clicked.

## Decision
The KRA row is now a disclosure. Its KPI list renders as an indented, tinted
panel immediately beneath that row, inside the same list; the trailing KPI card
is removed. Clicking the open KRA collapses it (`onSelectKra` accepts `null`),
and only one KRA is open at a time. The panel carries its own `KPIs · n` header
with the Employees / Weightage / Avg score column labels and scrolls internally
past ~420px. KPI row content — badges, "Fix text split", variants, "Open" — is
unchanged.

## Consequences
- Presentation only: no data loading, RPC, scoring or drawer behaviour changes.
- The KRA virtualizer measures rows via `measureElement`, so the taller expanded
  row is handled; the collapsed estimate stays 56px.
- Regression guard: `src/components/admin/bu-console/consoleLayout.test.tsx`.

## Rollback
Restore the trailing KPI `Card` block and revert the two components.
