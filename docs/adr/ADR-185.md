# ADR-185 — Re-opened Annual Reviews require an explicit terminal re-submit

- **Date:** 2026-07-27
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Policy:** POLICY §AR-REOPEN-REQUIRES-TERMINAL-RESUBMIT
- **Reported as:** "100508 and 101676 are Pending even though the BU Head review is completed (as per data)."

## Context

Two instances in the 2025-2026 cycle sat at `pending_bu` while reports showed a
full set of BU Head scores:

| Employee | Instance | BU Head draft | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100508 Satyam Kumar Jha | `8dbb50b3…` | 19/19 criteria, weighted 195, **unlocked** | `pending_bu` |
| 101676 Satyaban Roy | `3fde892d…` | 19/19 criteria, weighted 231, **unlocked** | `pending_bu` |

Stage-transition history (notification metadata, `type='annual_review_stage'`):

```text
101676:  25-Jul 10:27:34  completed    -> pending_bu     (re-opened)
         25-Jul 10:27:51  pending_bu   -> pending_dept   (BU sent back to Dept)
         25-Jul 10:30:14  pending_dept -> pending_bu     (Dept re-submitted)

100508:  25-Jul 13:07:21  completed    -> pending_bu     (re-opened)
         25-Jul 13:07:42  pending_bu   -> pending_dept   (BU sent back to Dept)
         26-Jul 04:11:11  pending_dept -> pending_bu     (Dept re-submitted)
```

## Root cause (5 Why)

1. Why pending? The `bu_head` response was not locked and had no `submitted_at`.
2. Why unlocked? The completed review was re-opened, which correctly unlocks the
   terminal response and clears the aggregates.
3. Why did it look complete? Re-open/send-back **preserves** the reviewer's
   entered scores (send-back data preservation), so the grid and report keep
   rendering BU scores.
4. Why did nobody re-submit? After the BU sent it back and the Dept Head
   re-submitted, the instance returned to the BU Head's queue as an already
   filled form; no fresh submit was performed.
5. Why was that invisible? No UI surface distinguishes "scored and submitted"
   from "scored draft, unlocked" for the terminal stage.

This is correct workflow behaviour, not a data-integrity defect. Scope check:
these were the only two instances system-wide at `pending_bu` with a fully
scored, unlocked `bu_head` response.

## Decision

1. Finalise both instances from the preserved BU drafts (admin decision), using
   `annual_review_compute_final_summary` — the same routine the normal submit
   path uses — so aggregates are derived, never hand-computed or zeroed.
2. Snapshot the complete before-state into
   `annual_review_bu_draft_finalise_2026_07` (admin-read only, RLS enabled) to
   keep the action reversible and auditable. `performed_by = NULL` marks it as a
   system-applied repair (§System performer attribution).
3. Codify the state itself in `src/lib/annualReview/reopenTerminalSignoff.ts` as
   the SSOT for classifying a preserved-but-unsubmitted terminal draft.

## Outcome

| Employee | Status | Criteria weighted | Total | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 101676 Satyaban Roy | completed | 231.00 | 58.20 | Average |
| 100508 Satyam Kumar Jha | completed | 195.00 | 51.00 | Poor |

Post-check detector (`pending_bu` + unlocked scored `bu_head` response) returns
**0** rows.

## CAPA

- **Corrective:** the two instances finalised, audited and reversible.
- **Preventive:** `resolveTerminalSignoffState` + `terminalSignoffLabel` give
  every surface one way to render "Scored draft — awaiting re-submit"; regression
  coverage in `src/test/annualReview/buDraftFinalise.test.ts` locks the rule that
  a scored-but-unlocked terminal response is **never** treated as completed and
  that finalising derives from the preserved draft.

## Rollback

`annual_review_bu_draft_finalise_2026_07` holds the prior status, lock flag,
`submitted_at` and aggregates; restoring both instances to `pending_bu` and
unlocking the two `bu_head` responses is a single update.
