# ADR-230 — Report-wide effective outcome parity (calibration + exemption)

- Status: Accepted (2026-08-01)
- Related: ADR-212 (rating slabs), ADR-220 (calibration), ADR-221/222/222a/224
  (eligibility, exemptions, increment penalty), ADR-228 (exemption-aware band placement)

## Context
Calibration (ADR-220) and the exemption penalty (ADR-222/224) were honoured by the Bell Curve
tab and the Detail tab, but the **Comprehensive tab**, the **main Excel workbook** and the
**Rating distribution chart** still derived the rating and slab straight from `total_score`
via `toRatingOutOf5()` / `resolveSlabPercent()`. The same employee could therefore read 4.6 /
20% on one tab and 3.2 / 8% on another. The Detail tab also built its `SlabCapOptions` inline
and omitted the ADR-224 penalty fields, a second drift source.

## Decision
1. **One resolver.** `src/lib/annualReview/reportRating.ts` is the SSOT for a report row's
   effective outcome: `resolveReportRating(row, ctx)` returns `computedRating`,
   `effectiveRating` (calibration applied), `calibratedRating`, `calibrationReason`,
   `eligibilityStatus`, `rawSlabPercent`, `slabPercent` (post `effectiveSlabPercent`) and
   `capApplied`. It composes the existing SSOTs — `effectiveRating()`, `resolveEligibility()`,
   `effectiveSlabPercent()` — and never re-implements them.
2. **One context builder.** `buildSlabCapOptions(bellCurveConfig, slabs)` carries the full
   ADR-224 penalty rule (mode, step-down count, scope, top-slab window, floor). Both the
   Detail tab and the Comprehensive tab now use it; the inline literal is deleted.
3. **Comprehensive tab.** Grid, drill-down cards and the Rating distribution chart show the
   effective rating and effective slab %, with `Cal` and `Capped` badges (tooltips carry the
   computed value, the pre-penalty %, and the reason/rule). The Final Rating band re-bands off
   the calibrated rating.
4. **Workbook.** The Employees sheet emits `Final Rating (out of 5)` and `Slab %` as the
   effective values, plus audit columns `Computed Rating (/5)`, `Calibrated Rating`,
   `Calibration Reason`, `Raw Slab %`, `Exemption Cap Applied` and `Eligibility Status`. The
   Rating Distribution sheet buckets on the effective band; the Executive Summary states the
   penalty rule in force.
5. **Template override respected.** Eligibility questions resolve from
   `template_override_id ?? template_id` (ADR-117), so overridden employees are assessed
   against the template they actually answered.
6. **Reversible.** `ExportInput.ratingContext` is optional; omitting it reproduces the
   pre-ADR-230 workbook byte-for-byte.

## Consequences
- Bell Curve, Detail, Comprehensive and both exports report one number per employee.
- Presentation layer only — no schema, RPC, RLS or stored-value change; `total_score` and
  `final_rating` in the database remain the raw, immutable computed values.
- Guard: `src/test/annualReview/reportRating.test.ts` (14 cases, incl. an anti-drift check).
