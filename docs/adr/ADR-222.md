# ADR-222: Exemption increment cap (exempted employees excluded from the top slab tiers)

- Status: Accepted (July 2026)
- Related: ADR-212 (rating slabs), ADR-218/218a-e (Bell Curve), ADR-220 (calibration), ADR-221 (eligibility & exemptions)

## Context

ADR-221 lets Admin/HR/Management waive an operational eligibility failure (absent days, LWP) so the employee becomes `Exempted (Eligible)` and receives their computed increment slab in full. Business policy is that an exempted employee should still not access the top increment tiers, and how many tiers are withheld must be configurable per cycle — not hardcoded.

## Decision

1. **Cycle master data.** `annual_review_bell_curve_config` gains `exempted_slab_cap_enabled boolean default true` and `exempted_top_tiers_excluded integer default 2` (CHECK 0..6). No tier count or percentage is hardcoded anywhere.
2. **Cap resolution SSOT.** `slabCapPercent(slabs, n)` in `src/lib/annualReview/ratingSlab.ts` returns the highest `increment_percent` remaining after removing the top `n` active slab tiers (sorted by percent). Excluding every tier yields `0`.
3. **Clamping.** `effectiveSlabPercent(percent, status, capOptions)` clamps only `exempted` rows to that ceiling when the cap is enabled. `eligible` / `unknown` are untouched; `ineligible` remains 0% (ADR-221 decision A). `isSlabCapped()` reports whether the clamp actually lowered the value and drives the "Capped" badge.
4. **Ratings unchanged.** The cap affects the increment percentage only — never the /5 rating, the band assignment, the bell-curve distribution or calibration.
5. **Surfaces.** Config lives in `BellCurveConfigDialog` (switch + tier count with a live "max %" hint). The active rule is echoed in the Bell Curve header, applied in `BandEmployeeList` (badge + CSV), in the Bell Curve Excel/PDF exports (`Exemption Cap Applied` column) and in the Annual Review Report grid and its Excel export, so every surface reports the same number.

## Consequences

- Additive schema; rollback = drop the two columns and revert the touched client files. With the cap disabled the behaviour is exactly ADR-221.
- Changing the tier count re-prices exempted employees everywhere at once — intentional, and audited through the existing bell-curve config trail.
- Guard: `src/test/annualReview/effectiveEligibility.test.ts` (17 cases, 8 new for the cap).
