# ADR-086 — Multi-month re-percolation: relax status guard & support cross-year cycles

**Date:** 2026-06-10
**Status:** Accepted
**Supersedes:** ADR-047 (POLICY §54 v4)

## Context

Ashish Kataria's 6-Monthly (Half-Yearly Nov 2025 – Apr 2026) KPI was edited
by an Auditor in April 2026 via the admin-data-entry path. The terminal April
submission updated, but the five sibling months (Nov 2025 – Mar 2026) kept
stale scores. No `SCORE_REPERCOLATED` audit rows were emitted.

RCA found two latent defects in `repercolate_on_submission_update`:

- **Defect A — Status guard too strict.** Admin paths (`admin_bulk_apply`,
  `ADMIN_DATA_ENTRY_AUDITOR`) update `review_submissions` while
  `kpis.status` is still `submitted` (mid-transition). The trigger bailed at
  `IF v_kpi.status != 'approved'` and never propagated. The subsequent
  `status -> approved` transition fired `percolate_multimonth_score`, but if
  the row was already approved by that point the transition was a no-op.
- **Defect B — Cross-year cycles dropped.** Sibling lookup used
  `AND k.review_year = v_kpi.review_year`. For Nov 2025 – Apr 2026 (terminal
  April 2026, year 2026), the Nov/Dec 2025 siblings were silently filtered out.

## Decision

1. Re-percolate when **either** `kpis.status = 'approved'` **or** the terminal
   submission already carries `NEW.final_score IS NOT NULL`. A locked final
   score is the authoritative signal that the cycle's score is canonical,
   independent of in-flight `kpis.status` transitions.
2. Resolve each sibling's calendar year from its position in the cycle:
   wrapping cycles (have both Dec and Jan) place months with canonical index
   strictly greater than the terminal index in `terminal_year - 1`; all other
   months stay in `terminal_year`. Removes the hard-coded `review_year`
   equality and uses an inline CTE to join the cycle membership.
3. Audit metadata stamps `policy = 'POLICY_54_v5'`, plus `sibling_period`,
   `sibling_year`, and `source_year` for forensic clarity.

## Consequences

- Admin edits on terminal months now propagate even mid-transition.
- Half-Yearly, Quarterly, Bi-Monthly, and Yearly cycles that wrap a calendar
  boundary fan out across both years.
- No behavior change for non-terminal edits (still no-op).
- Recursion guard (`app.repercolation_active`) unchanged.

## Backfill

Historical multi-month KPIs whose terminal submission was edited
post-approval without sibling propagation will be repaired by a follow-up
`BACKFILL_MULTIMONTH_PERCOLATION_v2` job (separate migration).
