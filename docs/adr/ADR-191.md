# ADR-191 — Email preheader hygiene and finalized-score integrity

**Date:** 2026-07-28
**Status:** Accepted

## Context
Jaspal received a finalized-KPI email with two defects visible in the screenshot:
1. The inbox snippet showed a raw backend storage URL.
2. The subject/body read **"Score: N/A/5"** for KPIs that were properly approved with a real score.

## 5 Whys

**Gap 1 — backend URL in the snippet**
1. Why is a storage URL in the preview line? → Mail clients derive the snippet from the first content of the message.
2. Why is that a URL? → The first element in `<body>` is the branding `<img src="https://<backend>/storage/v1/object/public/branding-assets/…">`.
3. Why does the client fall back to the image URL? → No preheader element and no plain-text alternative were supplied.
4. Why no text part? → `sendViaSmtp` / `sendViaResend` were written HTML-only.
5. Root cause: **the email builder never controlled the snippet surface.**

**Gap 2 — "N/A/5"**
1. Why N/A? → `{{final_score}}` resolved to the literal `N/A`.
2. Why? → `placeholderData.final_score` fell back to `'N/A'` because `final_score` was absent from the request body.
3. Why absent? → `send_email_on_notification()` built the payload without `final_score`.
4. Why? → The score lives on `review_submissions`, not `kpis`/`notifications`, and the trigger only joined `kpis`.
5. Root cause: **the notification→email payload contract omitted the score's source of truth**, and the renderer degraded to a misleading string instead of omitting the clause.

## Decision
1. `buildEmailHtml()` emits a hidden preheader `<div>` first; `buildPreheaderText()` derives it from the message body and strips URLs.
2. Every send path attaches a plain-text alternative (`buildPlainTextEmail()`), body-derived, no URLs.
3. `send_email_on_notification()` resolves `final_score` / `is_na` from `review_submissions` for `final_approved` events only, preferring `metadata.final_score`. Wrapped in its own `EXCEPTION` block.
4. `resolveFinalScoreDisplay()` classifies the score as resolved / N/A / missing. Missing ⇒ the score clause is **removed** from the subject and body. `N/A/5` is now unrepresentable.
5. Templates are cloned before mutation (`DEFAULT_TEMPLATES` was being edited in place across warm-isolate invocations).

## Consequences
- Additive only: no schema, RLS, or table changes; non-`final_approved` events keep their exact payload and rendering.
- Emails degrade to "Your KPI Has Been Finalized" (no score clause) if the lookup fails — never a wrong number.
- Guarded by `src/tests/emailFinalScoreFormat.test.ts`; policy §EMAIL-SCORE-AND-PREHEADER.
