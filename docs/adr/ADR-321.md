# ADR-321 — Group edit: forward months from a past anchor, and text-only standardisation

Status: Accepted · 2026-08

## Context

Two gaps surfaced in the Performance Console group definition edit:

1. With the console parked on Jul 2026 (past, current month Aug 2026),
   `spanModesAvailable()` collapsed the ADR-291 **Apply to** control to
   `This month only`. An admin correcting a definition "from July onwards" had
   to repeat the edit month by month.
2. A Title + Description edit reported 12 skipped employees
   (`final_score_locked`, `past_kra_set`). `bu_console_group_edit_definition`
   decided skip/write before looking at *which* fields changed, so a pure
   wording correction was blocked by scoring immutability it cannot affect.

## Decision

### Span from a past anchor

The invariant is restated: *never write a past month the admin did not
explicitly select*. All three modes are offered whatever the anchor. A forward
or next-N span from a past anchor resolves to the anchor itself plus the months
from the current calendar month onward; the months in between are excluded and
the exclusion is stated in the dialog. Cap, per-month dry-run, sequential
commit, per-month `bu_console_edit_runs` and per-month undo are unchanged.

### Text-only standardisation

`public.bu_console_descriptive_fields()` is the SSOT for fields with no scoring
or structural meaning: `kpi_title`, `kpi_description`, `criteria`,
`source_of_data`, `kpi_formula`, `kpi_scoring_logic`, `uom`. Mirrored client-side
in `src/components/admin/bu-console/editFieldClass.ts` for UI guidance only.

`bu_console_group_edit_definition` gains `p_text_only boolean DEFAULT false`.
When set:

- the caller must be an admin, and the server re-derives the classification from
  `p_changes`, raising if any non-descriptive field is present;
- `final_score_locked` and `past_kra_set` stop being skip reasons;
- `individual_override` and `cycle_anchor_conflict` still skip;
- the run row records `text_only`, and each `kpi_audit_logs` entry carries the
  flag, so the rewrite stays auditable and undoable.

POLICY §88 is narrowed, not bypassed: the approved final score, its inputs and
the workflow status remain immutable — only wording changes.

## Consequences

Additive parameter and one nullable-safe column on `bu_console_edit_runs`. Both
behaviours are opt-in; with the toggle off and mode `This month only` the
behaviour is identical to before.

Tests: `src/test/groupEditSpan.test.ts` (past anchor honoured, no implicit past
month, cap), `src/test/editFieldClass.test.ts` (per-field classification).
