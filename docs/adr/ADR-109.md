# ADR-109 — BU Head is a terminal reviewer (no dept_head routing)

Date: 2026-07-17
Status: Accepted

## Context

A Business Unit Head is organizationally senior to every Department Head under
their BU. Their own Annual Review instance was inheriting `dept_head` in
`enabled_stages` (with `dept_head_id` pointing to a subordinate), so after
Self the form was routing to `pending_dept` — a hierarchy inversion where a
junior employee reviews the BU Head.

Live examples (active cycle, before repair):

- Sajid Raza (100264, BU 1050 TPD / 3X100 TPD / DRI) → dept_head Jyoti Prakash Dwivedi.
- Jitendra Kumar Dwivedi (101148, BU 45 MW / 8 MW) → dept_head Satyendra Kumar Singh.
- Abhas Luharuwalla (100856, BU Commercial-Plant) → **already stuck at `pending_dept`** with Atul Kumar Khaitan as reviewer.

The Jaspal case (ADR-105 duplicate-reviewer dedup) worked accidentally because
he happened to be both dept and BU head — dedup collapsed the two into one.
That rule does not cover BU Heads whose configured dept head is a distinct
(junior) person.

## Decision (POLICY §AR-BU-HEAD-TERMINAL)

If an employee is themselves a BU Head, strip `dept_head` from their own
instance's `enabled_stages` and null `dept_head_id`. Chain becomes:

```text
Self → BU Head → HR   (or Self → HR / Self only, per enabled_stages)
```

## Implementation

1. `public.is_bu_head(uuid)` — stable helper.
2. `annual_review_bu_head_terminal_diagnostic(cycle)` — read-only preview.
3. `repair_bu_head_terminal_chains(cycle, dry_run)` — idempotent repair;
   audit rows in `annual_review_bu_head_terminal_audit_2026_07`; advances
   `pending_dept` → `pending_bu` / `pending_hr` / `completed`.
4. `tg_business_units_apply_terminal_rule` — AFTER INSERT/UPDATE OF
   `head_user_id` on `business_units` re-applies the rule to the new BU
   head's own open instances.
5. `tg_departments_cascade_head_to_ar` extended to skip BU-head employees.
6. TS mirror in `src/lib/annualReview/effectiveChain.ts` accepts
   `employeeIsBuHead` and emits `skipReason='bu_head_terminal'`.
7. One-shot migration repair on the active cycle (20 instances patched).

## Consequences

- Reviewer queues of wrongly-mapped dept heads shrink (correct).
- Fully rollback-able via the audit table.
- `completed` instances are never mutated.
