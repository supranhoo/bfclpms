# ADR-210: Reset rollback from `annual_review_reset_archive`

## Status
**Accepted**

## Date
2026-07-31

## Context
Employee 101885 (Chandan Kumar Pandit) had a completed self-review, locked on
2026-07-17, with the instance sitting at `pending_bu`. On 2026-07-31 an admin
ran a force-reset with the reason "EXCLUDED CHANDAN". The reset wiped the
locked self response, swapped `template_id` from `a6e88cd5…` to `eb87efa6…`
("Generic W - (With KRA)") and re-anchored the instance to `pending_self`.
`annual_review_responses` for that instance was left empty — the self-review
content survived only inside `annual_review_reset_archive.wiped_responses`.

Force-reset is intentionally destructive, but it is also intentionally
recoverable: the archive row captures `prior_status`, `prior_template_id` and
the verbatim response payload. There was no defined rollback path, so operators
were re-typing answers or leaving reviews stranded at `pending_self`.

## Decision
1. `annual_review_reset_archive` is the **authoritative rollback source** for a
   force-reset. Responses are re-inserted verbatim (same `id`, `submitted_at`,
   `created_at`, `is_locked`) so the audit story stays continuous.
2. A rollback MUST also restore `prior_template_id`. Archived answer keys are
   template-scoped; restoring responses under a swapped template orphans them.
3. `overall_status` MUST be re-anchored, never left at `pending_self`. The
   archived `prior_status` wins when its stage is still in the instance's
   current `enabled_stages`; otherwise the first enabled stage without a
   restored locked response is used, else `completed`.
4. **Reviewer mappings are never rolled back.** Remaps made after the reset
   (including `annual_review_assignment_overrides`) are newer intent and win.
5. Every rollback snapshots before/after into a dated repair table with
   `performed_by = NULL` (system-run repair). The archive row is left intact.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative A: Keep the new template and remap answers onto it
- **Rejected because:** questions with no counterpart in the new template would
  be silently dropped, losing content the employee actually wrote.

### Alternative B: Restore the answers as an unlocked draft at `pending_self`
- **Rejected because:** the stage was genuinely submitted and locked; forcing a
  re-submit misrepresents the timeline and delays the BU Head.

### Alternative C: Rebuild by asking the employee to re-enter the review
- **Rejected because:** the data exists; re-entry destroys the original
  `submitted_at` and the immutable audit narrative.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Erroneous resets are fully recoverable with the original timestamps intact.
- Reviews return to the correct queue instead of stalling at `pending_self`.

### Negative
- The restored template may be older than the currently-assigned one; a
  deliberate template change must be re-applied after the rollback.

### Neutral
- Applied 2026-07-31 to instance `febfb82a…` (101885): self response
  `5846a084…` restored, template reverted to `a6e88cd5…`, status set to
  `pending_bu`, BU Head `79fe4ca0…` preserved. Audit row in
  `annual_review_self_restore_repair_2026_07`.
