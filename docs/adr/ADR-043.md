# ADR-043: Org KPI Audit Review Governance

## Status
**Accepted**

## Date
2026-03-31

## Context
Organization-level KPIs with audit stages in their workflow needed a dedicated review interface for auditors, separate from individual employee KPI audit flows.

## Decision
A dedicated Org KPI Audit Review page (`/admin/org-kpi-audit-review`) shows only org-level KPIs whose employee instances have reached audit-reviewable status. Auditor scores written to `review_submissions`. Bulk approve applies a single score to all pending employees under one org KPI. Access restricted to Auditor and Admin roles.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative A: Use the existing AuditScorecard for org KPIs
- **Description:** Mix org KPIs into the per-employee audit queue
- **Rejected because:** Org KPIs may have hundreds of employee instances. Reviewing them individually in the standard audit queue is impractical and loses the organizational context.

### Alternative B: Auto-approve org KPIs at propagation time
- **Description:** Skip audit for org-level KPIs entirely
- **Rejected because:** Violates workflow policy — if audit is configured in the workflow, it must be performed. Skipping undermines compliance.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Efficient bulk auditing of org-level KPIs
- Preserves individual workflow compliance per employee

### Negative
- Additional page and menu entry to maintain

### Neutral
- Uses same scoring patterns as `AuditScorecard.tsx`
